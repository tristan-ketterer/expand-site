What Expand is

Expand helps you identify a limiting belief, examine the evidence for it, write a more expansive rival belief, and test it in the real world with small, personalized experiments.

Expand is a personal-growth and self-reflection tool. It is not therapy or medical care, and it doesn't treat, diagnose, or heal anything. If you're going through something heavy, please bring a human into it — the app always points to support resources, including the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (US) and findahelpline.com.
