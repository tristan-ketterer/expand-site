Privacy Policy

Effective August 4, 2026

Expand only works if you can be honest inside it. This policy explains exactly what happens to what you write — in the same plain language the app uses.

What we collect

  • Account information: your email address (or Apple Sign-In identifier) and an internal account ID, used to sign you in and keep your data yours.
  • Your content: the beliefs, conversations, evidence, experiments, check-in answers, journals, and profile facts you create in the app.
  • Notifications: a device push token, if you allow notifications, used only to deliver your check-ins and reminders.
  • Minimal usage events: small internal counters (for example, that a milestone was celebrated) used to make the app work. No third-party analytics, no advertising identifiers, no tracking across other apps or websites.

Where it lives

Your data is stored with Supabase (our database provider), encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest (AES-256). Every record is locked to your account with row-level security — the app can only ever read yours.

AI processing

To generate the app's replies, your relevant content (your messages, and the belief work it refers to) is sent to Anthropic, our AI provider, and processed under their commercial API terms. It is used only to generate your reply — not to train AI models — and is not shared with anyone else.

What we never do

  • No selling or renting your data. Ever.
  • No advertising, no ad networks, no third-party analytics or trackers.
  • No use of your content for AI training.

Retention & deletion

Your data is kept for as long as you keep your account, so the app can hold your history for you. You can export everything as a PDF at any time (Settings → Share profile), and you can permanently delete your account and every trace of your data at any time (Settings → Delete account & all data). Deletion is immediate and irreversible — nothing is retained.

Not therapy

Expand is a personal-growth and self-reflection tool. It is not therapy or medical care and does not treat, diagnose, or heal anything. If you're in crisis, the app surfaces support resources, including the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (US) and findahelpline.com.

Children

Expand is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll say so in the app before the change applies to you.
